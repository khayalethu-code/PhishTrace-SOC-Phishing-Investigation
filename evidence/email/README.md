# Email Evidence

This directory documents sanitized email evidence collected during the PhishTrace investigation.

## Planned Evidence

- Email overview and relevant metadata
- Sanitized email header extracts
- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication results, when available
- Notes about the controlled test URL

## Evidence Handling

- Record the evidence source and collection timestamp.
- Keep original email files and unredacted headers private.
- Remove personal email addresses and unrelated personal information before publishing.
- Do not upload passwords, tokens, private messages, or confidential data.
- Record actual observations only; do not invent evidence.
